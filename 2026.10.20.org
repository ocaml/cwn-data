* opentelemetry 0.92
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 1
:END:
Archive: https://discuss.ocaml.org/t/ann-opentelemetry-0-92/18619/1

** Simon Cruanes announced


Hello, opentelemetry 0.92 was [[https://github.com/ocaml-tracing/ocaml-opentelemetry/releases/tag/v0.92][just released]]. It is an OCaml implementation of the https://opentelemetry.io/ standard for observability. This release improves performance of random generation of trace Ids/span Ids, and adds support for head sampling. We are getting close to a 1.0 release.

Cheerios!
