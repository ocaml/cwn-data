opentelemetry 0.92
══════════════════

  Archive: <https://discuss.ocaml.org/t/ann-opentelemetry-0-92/18619/1>


Simon Cruanes announced
───────────────────────

  Hello, opentelemetry 0.92 was [just released]. It is an OCaml
  implementation of the <https://opentelemetry.io/> standard for
  observability. This release improves performance of random generation
  of trace Ids/span Ids, and adds support for head sampling. We are
  getting close to a 1.0 release.

  Cheerios!
